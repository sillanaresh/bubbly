Your desktop companion

Bubbly

A soft little presence that lives on your Mac.

Bubbly floats while you work, reacts when you click it, keeps you company, and opens into a lightweight chat assistant when you need a quick answer.

Download for Mac Early tester build for friends.

The experience

Present, playful, never in the way.

Bubbly gently breathes, blinks, bobs, and wanders around your desktop. Drag it anywhere, pause it, hide it, or let it quietly drift beside your work.

Chat with Bubbly

Ask a quick question without opening another big app.

Make it yours

Choose how much personality you want.

Bubbly can stay calm, become a tiny chat companion, or bring playful little effects onto the desktop for a quick break. Playtime adds rain, clouds, butterflies, sparkles, and cannon bursts; themes and moods change how Bubbly looks and feels.

Carefree

Just Bubbly floating around, with no extra action icons.

Chatty

Open a compact chat panel when you want a quick answer.

Playtime

Trigger rain, clouds, butterflies, sparkles, and cannon bursts.

Bubbly Max

Chat plus every playful effect around the bubble.

Themes and moods

Change colors, expressions, sounds, characters, and motion style so Bubbly feels like it belongs on your screen.

Right-click controls

Right-click Bubbly to show, hide, pause, reset position, switch modes, change themes, and quit cleanly.

Local-first feel

Preferences stay on your Mac. Chat only uses the network when you send a message.
