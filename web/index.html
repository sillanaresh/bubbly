Mac desktop companion

A small bubble that stays nearby.

A soft little presence that lives on your Mac. Bubbly floats while you work, reacts when you click it, keeps you company, and opens into a lightweight chat assistant when you need a quick answer.

It behaves like a desktop thing, not another tab.

Present, playful, never in the way. Bubbly gently breathes, blinks, bobs, and wanders around your desktop.

01

Drift while you work

Drag it anywhere, pause it, hide it, change how it looks, or let it quietly drift beside your work.

02

Click for a tiny reaction

A quick bounce and soft sound make the companion feel present without turning your desktop into a toy box.

03

Open chat beside it

Chat pins Bubbly in place and opens a lightweight panel for quick questions, ideas, and friendly back-and-forth. Closing chat lets Bubbly return to wandering.

Pick the amount of personality.

Bubbly can stay calm, become a tiny chat companion, or bring playful little effects onto the desktop for a quick break. Playtime adds rain, clouds, butterflies, sparkles, and cannon bursts; themes and moods change how Bubbly looks and feels.

Carefree

Just Bubbly floating around, with no extra action icons.

Chatty

Open a compact chat panel when you want a quick answer.

Playtime

Trigger rain, clouds, butterflies, sparkles, and cannon bursts.

Bubbly Max

Chat plus every playful effect around the bubble.

Small controls, visible choices.

The goal is not endless settings. The goal is enough personality that Bubbly feels like yours.

Themes and moods

Change colors, expressions, sounds, characters, and motion style so Bubbly feels like it belongs on your screen.

Right-click controls

Right-click Bubbly to show, hide, pause, reset position, switch modes, change themes, and quit cleanly.

Local-first feel

Preferences stay on your Mac. Chat only uses the network when you send a message.

Try the early Mac build.

This is an early friend build, so Bubbly is not Apple-notarized yet. Apple notarization needs a paid Apple Developer account, which costs about $99/year.

If macOS blocks it, click Done, then open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and use Open Anyway for Bubbly. Do not choose Move to Bin.
